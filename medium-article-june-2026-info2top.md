# Medium Article Signposts: Information to Topology (v1.4.0)

**Theme:** We didn't invent the topology of thought; we recovered it. 

### 1. The Trap: Hairballs & The Linear Prison
* **Point A:** Books force 3D thought into 1D lines. Databases force n-ary reality into 2D dyadic pairs (Euler's legacy).
* **Point B:** When we try to visualize complexity, we get the "undifferentiated hairball."
* **The Hook:** We blame human cognition for failing to read the hairball. The real problem is our rendering engines are collapsing separate dimensions of logic into one flat plane.

### 2. The Proof: Independent Recovery (The 4 Categories)
* **Point A:** We categorize all relations into four primitives: State Change, Containment, Interactivity, Reference. 
* **Point B:** *Crucial Pivot* — We didn't invent this taxonomy. It’s an independent recovery of human cognitive structure.
* **Point C (The Receipts):**
    * Roger Schank's Conceptual Dependency (ATRANS, PTRANS, MTRANS).
    * Aristotle's Categories (Place, Relation, Acting/Being-Acted-Upon).
    * 2026 Compression-driven AI (MDL recovered these exact primitives from raw data).

### 3. The Razor: The Constitutive-Descriptive Test
* **Point A:** How do we adjudicate Interactivity vs Reference without subjective bias? 
* **Point B:** *The Cut Test:* Sever the edge. Do the endpoints change?
    * *Constitutive (Interactivity):* Creates a role that dies with the edge (husband, bonded atom, runtime dependency).
    * *Descriptive (Reference):* Points without altering (citation, unrequited lover, static import).
* **Point C:** Note the symmetry sub-fork (Aristotle’s *poiein/paschein* — symmetric vs asymmetric roles).

### 4. The Discipline: The Firebreak (Edges vs Incidences)
* **Point A:** The "Central Theoretical Result": Edges Report, Incidences Interpret.
* **Point B:** Edges are pure empirical anchors (the text itself). Incidences are where subjective opinions live.
* **Point C:** The Firebreak keeps the methodology testable and stops metaphysical/ontological interpretation from polluting the evidence layer. 

### 5. The Horizon: Legibility & The Renderer
* **Point A:** Refuting the "humans can't read graphs" myth. 
* **Point B:** Look at transit maps, family trees, org charts. They are highly legible because they are *single-layer projections*. 
* **Point C:** The future (and the roadmap) isn't better lines, but a hyperedge-native renderer: enclosing bounds for Containment, shared fields for Interactivity, gradients for State Change. 

### 6. The Outro / Teaser
* **Point A:** By shedding the dyadic constraint, we reclaim an ancient relational heritage.
* **Point B:** Drop a hook for the next frontier: Is "Typing/Instantiation" a degenerate Containment or a true 5th category?
