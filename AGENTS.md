# Information to Topology, v1.4.0 Changeset

This file is a delta against v1.3.0, not a replacement. Each addition names its target section and is written in the paper’s voice for direct integration. Nothing here revises a prior claim, the five additions extend the typology’s adjudication, ground the four categories in independent prior art, open one new category question, and supply empirical support for the renderer roadmap. A sixth section marks the material that the firebreak keeps out of this paper, and says why.

The five integrable additions are evidence-side. The held-out material is interpretation-side. That partition is itself an application of the paper’s Central Theoretical Result.

-----

## Addition 1, Section 9, the constitutive-descriptive rule for Interactivity

*Target: extends the boundary-edge question in Section 9, and supplies the adjudication rule that question says is missing.*

Section 9 records that the category boundaries producing the flagged review set are not yet governed by adjudication rules. One of those boundaries, Reference against Interactivity, resolves under a single checkable predicate.

An Interactivity edge is constitutive, it confers roles on both endpoints that exist only while the edge holds, and it brings a relational fact into being that was not present before. A Reference edge is descriptive, it leaves both endpoints’ types unchanged and reports a relation that already held. The test is cut the edge and inspect the endpoints. If an endpoint loses a role-identity that was real only through the relation, husband, employee, creditor, plaintiff, bonded atom, the edge was constitutive and classifies as Interactivity. If both endpoints stand unchanged, the lover who loves a beloved who need never know, the citation that points at a source indifferent to being cited, the edge was descriptive and classifies as Reference. The roles of a constitutive edge are the two ends of the relation, not properties either node carries alone, which is why the edge cannot be one-sided and why severing it deletes a fact rather than merely a pointer.

This rule also dissolves one of the view-dependent cases Section 9 raised as open. An `imports` relation reads as static Reference in a dependency listing and as active Interactivity at runtime, and the reason is now exact, not ad hoc. At rest the relation points, it confers no role and alters nothing, it is descriptive. At runtime it brings a live dependency-satisfaction relation into being, a role each end holds only for the duration, it is constitutive. The view-dependence is therefore not a defect in the predicate, it is the predicate reporting that the static and dynamic views present genuinely different edges under one verb. The format can record the view, and the constitutive-descriptive test decides the category within each view rather than across them.

The possession-against-containment boundary partitions the same way. Possession standing confers no new role and alters nothing, it sorts as Reference or, where the language frames it spatially, Containment. The transfer of possession, the act that ends one possession relation and begins another, is the constitutive event, and sorts as Interactivity. The two were never one edge, the static relation and the act that changes it have always been different objects, which is the token-history-against-type-quotient distinction of Section 4 appearing again at the level of a single relation.

## Addition 2, Section 9, the symmetry sub-fork inside Interactivity

*Target: a new sub-question under the boundary-edge discussion in Section 9, adjacent to the dyadic-projection direction-attribute machinery.*

Constitutive edges divide by how the conferred roles sit on the two ends. Symmetric interactions assign the same role to both endpoints, marriage, alliance, covalent bond, peering, co-authorship, each end the same kind of party. Asymmetric interactions assign complementary unequal roles, hires against is-hired-by, lends against borrows-from, sues against is-sued-by, the symmetry broken into a role-pair. Whether this is a sub-type split inside Interactivity, or a symmetry attribute riding on a single category, is open.

The paper’s own design supplies a prior. Direction and weight already live as edge attributes rather than spawning categories, the dyadic-projection treatment carries them on the edge. Symmetry has the same shape and a strong prior to live there too, as an attribute on the Interactivity edge, unless a case can be shown where symmetric and asymmetric constitutive relations require different decomposition mathematics downstream, at which point the split earns category status by behaving differently under decomposition rather than by intuition.

A historical note bears on the seam’s reality rather than its resolution. Aristotle’s Categories cut here, treating acting, *poiein*, and being-acted-upon, *paschein*, as two distinct categories rather than as one directed relation with a sense. The first relational taxonomy in the Western canon factored the constitutive relation across its two role-ends, which is evidence the seam is structural enough to force a representational decision, independent of which way the present format resolves it.

## Addition 3, Sections 8 and a new Related Work section, the four categories as independently recovered

*Target: strengthens the Structural-Isomorphism Recovery Hypothesis in Section 8, and supplies the related-work grounding a peer-review document requires. The paper currently rests the “discovered, not posited” claim on the inter-annotator convergence test alone. The recurrence of the same cut across independent traditions is a second, external line of support.*

The four categories are not unique to this method. Several independent frameworks, built for different purposes, partition relations along the same joints, which is the kind of convergence the Structural-Isomorphism Recovery Hypothesis predicts.

Roger Schank’s Conceptual Dependency, 1969 through the late 1970s, decomposes events into primitive acts, three of which are the categories carried as verbs. ATRANS, the change of an abstract relationship such as possession or ownership, is constitutive Interactivity. PTRANS, the change of physical location, is State Change. MTRANS, the mental transfer of information, is Reference. Schank’s primitives are acts, transitions, not standing edges, which places his entire framework on the token-history side of the Section 4 distinction, the history without the type-quotient. This is independent corroboration that the history-against-transition-system axis is real, a second tradition having built only the history half.

Aristotle’s Categories give the cut older and split across the role-pair. Place is Containment, relation, *pros ti*, is Reference, acting and being-acted-upon together are Interactivity, and change is handled outside the categories, as *kinesis* in the Physics. That change sits outside the relational categories rather than among them is independent support for the suspicion this paper raises, that the static-against-changing axis is orthogonal to the category typology rather than one category among the four.

Jackendoff’s Conceptual Semantics runs on BE, GO, and CAUSE. GO, change along a path, is State Change, CAUSE is Interactivity, and his Thematic Relations Hypothesis, that the spatial schema is reused across abstract domains, is the Structural-Isomorphism Recovery Hypothesis approached from lexical semantics rather than from extraction.

WordNet and classical mereology supply Containment cleanly through meronymy, and their hyponymy, the is-a relation, is the typing edge that Addition 4 flags as a candidate fifth category, present here as a first-class relation in the most widely used lexical database.

A 2026 result bears directly on the objectivity claim. A compression-driven library learner, run over events represented as before-and-after state pairs and starting from four generic state-change primitives, recovered Schank’s ATRANS, PTRANS, and MTRANS from minimum-description-length pressure, and found the full inventory richer than Schank’s, with mental and emotional operators dominating physical ones in naturalistic data. The categories falling out of compression pressure rather than being stipulated is precisely the empirical shape the inter-annotator convergence test argues for from the human side. Two methods, compression and annotator agreement, both report the cut as found rather than imposed.

One caveat belongs in the same section, as a structural property of the evidence rather than an aside. This recurrence is demonstrable only across the traditions formalized into citation-dense, machine-readable form, Western analytic philosophy, mid-century symbolic AI, classical Greek, and the *Paṭṭhāna* already integrated in v1.1.0. Whether a Nyāya relational typology, a classical Chinese one, or other systems cut the four the same way is underdetermined by the available corpus, and that underdetermination is upstream in the substrate, a property of what was indexed, not evidence of absence. The convergence claim should therefore be scoped to the indexed traditions, with extraction over non-Western relational systems marked as the test that would either widen the claim or bound it.

## Addition 4, Section 9, typing as a candidate fifth category

*Target: a new open question in Section 9, and a flag on a possible unaxiomatized dependency in Section 4.*

The type-to-instance edge resists reduction to the four. It is not Containment, the class is not a region the instance sits inside, it is a pattern the instance satisfies. It is not Reference, the instance does not point at its type, it instantiates it. It is not State Change, nothing is altered, and it is not Interactivity, no role is conferred and nothing is brought into being. The relation between a thing and what kind of thing it is appears as a first-class relation, hyponymy, in WordNet, and as `rdf:type` and `subClassOf` in the standard ontology stack, which is weak evidence it does not fold into the four.

The question is whether typing is a genuine fifth category or a degenerate Containment, set membership read spatially, and whether the decision changes the decomposition mathematics. The question is not idle for this paper specifically, because the Section 4 type-quotient is an operation over a typing relation. Collapsing a token-history by type presupposes a fact about which tokens share a type, and that fact is exactly a type-to-instance edge. The paper may therefore already depend on a fifth relation it has not axiomatized, in the very construction that defines its newest layer. Resolving typing’s status is consequently entangled with whether the type-quotient is well-defined, not merely with the completeness of the typology.

## Addition 5, Section 8, legible single-layer diagrams as positive evidence

*Target: empirical support for the Structural-Isomorphism Recovery Hypothesis and for the hyperedge-native renderer being the correct next artifact. Reframes the apparent absence of legible graph viewers from a counterexample into corroboration.*

The objection that no graph viewer is legible to untrained humans, and that this absence indicates the structure is foreign to cognition, fails on available evidence. Legible graph-shaped diagrams are in wide public use and require no training, they are simply not filed under graph viewer. Transit maps are pure connection topology, Beck’s 1933 design discarding geographic distance to preserve adjacency, read by millions. Genealogies render Containment together with constitutive Interactivity and are followed across generations. Org charts, Sankey diagrams for flow and State Change, dependency parse trees, and Novak concept maps in classrooms are all graph-shaped and legible.

The property these share is the operative one. Each commits to a single relation type, two at most, and gives that type its own honest visual channel. They are single-layer projections, the `split_by_predicate` adapter run down to one layer and then stopped. Their legibility is therefore direct support for the Structural-Isomorphism Recovery Hypothesis, comprehension arrives exactly when one category is rendered in one channel without the other three drawn over it in the same plane.

This relocates the missing artifact and narrows it. What is absent is neither the human capacity, which these diagrams exercise constantly, nor per-type rendering, which exists in pockets and works. What is absent is the general renderer that holds several categories differentiated and co-visible at once without collapsing them back into one undifferentiated plane. The undifferentiated hairball is the multilayer dumped unsplit, the family tree is the multilayer split all the way down to one, and the untested middle, two or three categories held apart by distinct visual channels, is precisely what the hyperedge-native renderer specified in v1.1.0 is for. It has never had a faithful substrate on which to be tested, which is why the absence reads as a gap in cognition only if the legible single-layer cases are not counted. Counted, they make the renderer’s central bet the natural next step rather than a speculative one.

## Section 6 of this changeset, what the firebreak holds out of the paper

The conversation that produced the five additions above also developed an ontological reading, and that reading does not enter this paper. The reason is the firebreak, the paper’s Central Theoretical Result, applied to the paper’s own contents.

The reading, stated once and in full so the boundary is legible, runs as follows. The four categories are lossy projections of a single comprehensive relation, the untransduced edge between two nodes, which at full resolution exceeds any rendering of it. Source is definable as structure at zero transduction tax, the pre-image of which every legible graph is a projection. The physical twin of the comprehensive edge is the entangled bipartite state, whose information lives in the non-factorizable correlations rather than in either endpoint, and the physical pre-image of a probability field is the phase-carrying amplitude field, the Born rule being the first tax paid. Reification-by-reference generalizes to the claim that node-hood and edge-hood are positions rather than essences, that a vertex is an edge-cluster held still at a grain one has stopped resolving. The zero-tax limit is approachable and never occupiable, because occupying it would be a transduction and would reintroduce the tax.

This is coherent with the framework. It is also strictly interpretation-side. The methods paper makes evidence-side claims an extraction can test, convergence agreement, decomposition output, flagged-edge distribution. The ontology names an object that, by its own apophatic clause, no extraction can ever exhibit. Routing it into the paper would carry interpretation across the firebreak into the evidence layer, which is the one move the paper exists to forbid, and it would give a reviewer the lever to deflate the testable core by association with an untestable claim.

One sentence of the reading is admissible in the methods register, and only one. The realism fork the paper already stakes implicitly, whether the recovered topology is objective or whether the four categories are shared annotator habit, is the same fork as source-real against source-regulative. Stating that the paper takes the realist branch, and that the convergence test measures access to a structure presumed real rather than constructing the structure it measures, is a scoped methodological commitment that belongs in Section 8 or Section 9 as a single declared assumption. The development of why that branch, and what it commits one to about pre-projection objects, is a different document.

That document is named here and left unwritten, deliberately. Working title, [[source-without-tax]], a relational ontology, or alternatively folded into the physics-of-cognition chapters of the longer nonfiction framework. It is not drafted in this changeset because the disciplined act at this stage is to keep the firebreak intact and return to the testable work, not to ascend into the interpretation layer while the methods paper still has open evidence-side questions. The ontology will keep. The convergence fixture with genuine recurrence, Open Question A, will not write itself.
